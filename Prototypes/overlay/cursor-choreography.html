Input that moves like intent

When the agent holds the real cursor, raw HID speed reads as possession. Slowed, eased motion reads as a hand. Every action telegraphs itself: the target ring fills radially during a pre-action delay — the window in which ⌥⎋ cancels — then contracts, and only then does the click land.

Eased glide

Cubic-Bézier paths with slow-in/slow-out, ~1s per leg at default speed — deliberately slower than the daemon needs, exactly fast enough to follow. A soft trail marks where the cursor has been for late glances.

Charge-up ring

The radial fill is the interrupt window made visible: the click cannot land before the ring closes, so a human always has the full delay to press ⌥⎋. Contract-then-ripple confirms the exact landing point.

Legible typing

Text arrives at dictation speed with a pulsing caret in the agent color — the field visibly owned by the agent, not a paste that teleports in.
