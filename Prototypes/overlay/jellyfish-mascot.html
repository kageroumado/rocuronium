The agent is a jellyfish

It floats, it has no hands, it moves by pulsing and drifting — exactly how ghost input works. Bioluminescence is the state channel: dim when idle, glowing when thinking, pulsing toward the target when acting, amber when it needs a human. The bell-pulse and the trailing tentacles are the signature; it never walks and never hops.

Four states, one creature

drifting · dim glow · agent standing by

Contract, then glide. Acting is a bell pulse aimed at the target — the squash-and-stretch is the pre-action telegraph that replaced the ghost's glancing eyes. Thinking brightens the glow to cyan and hovers. Needs-you turns the light amber and curls the tentacles up: visibly holding, touching nothing.

Cursor companion

drifting beside the pointer, tentacles echoing the trail

It escorts the cursor, never rides it. A soft spring keeps it a hand's width above and behind the pointer, leaning into the direction of travel while the tentacles stream behind — motion history a human can read at a glance. The cyan trail is the cursor's own wake in the same bioluminescent language. When the human moves the real mouse, the jellyfish lets go and drifts off-screen.
