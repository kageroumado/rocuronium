The Cast

The species catalogue is gone. These are not specimens — they are moods, each built to say the same four things Bitjelly says: standing by, thinking, working, and needing you. A ghost, who is not a jellyfish at all; an aurora that holds weather inside its bell; and a sparkler that sends its light away down its legs.

That is the four. Bitjelly keeps its own page, being the only pixel one; the three vector ones are here. The shipped smooth jellyfish is retired. Each has a different light mechanism, which was the rule the last slot had to satisfy — a bloom, a curtain, a glyph and a run of beads stay apart at 46 pt, where four palettes would not.

Three were cut on the way here. Ember, a paper lantern, was the most legible of them and the least like the others — a warm opaque body in a cast built on light passing through water. Beacon, an oxblood bell with a cold light lapping its rim, read as a warning light rather than as somebody to have on a desktop. A fried egg jelly was cute and real and simply not wanted.

Flip the state on the rail; all three change at once. The strip at the bottom draws them at 46 pt, which is the only size that decides anything — a mascot that reads beautifully at 300 px and turns to soup in the menu bar is not a mascot.
