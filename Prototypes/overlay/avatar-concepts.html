Who is the ghost on screen?

The product's whole identity is a ghost: input that acts without a body. When the agent becomes visible, the ghost is the natural face — plus one abstract mark for people who want a presence signal without a character. Three candidates, all animated.

A

The Courier

a ghost that carries the cursor
Approve Archive

The ghost holds the pointer the way rocuronium holds input: hovering just behind it, never in front of the click point. Before each move its eyes glance to the next target — intent telegraphed a beat before motion, so a watching human is never surprised. Blinks while waiting. Disappears the instant the human touches the mouse.

B

The Resident

a corner creature with states
Idle — watching for work

Lives in a screen corner for the whole session, like a desk companion: gentle bob when idle, swaying with thought-dots while planning, leaning in with a pulse ring while acting. The state chip gives the same information in words. This is the menu-bar-icon idea grown a body — glanceable state, zero reading required.

C

The Sigil

an abstract mark, no character
breathing · agent standing by

For people who want no mascot: a core, a halo, and an orbiting arc. Breathing means standing by; the arc orbits while acting; the mark turns amber and holds still when the agent needs a human decision. Reads at 16 px in a menu bar and at 96 px in an overlay — the same mark everywhere, which is what makes it trustworthy.
